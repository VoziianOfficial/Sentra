Privacy Policy

How information may be handled

This Privacy Policy describes the information practices for Sentra, a provider matching website that helps users compare independent local security provider options. By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information you may provide

You may provide information when submitting a form, requesting provider options, contacting us, or interacting with the website. This information may include your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, property type, requested service category, and message details.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to requests, help route provider matching inquiries, communicate about submitted requests, improve website functionality, maintain records, and support website security and compliance.

3. Provider matching and independent providers

Sentra is not a security contractor and does not perform installation, repair, guard, or monitoring services directly. Information submitted through the website may be used to help connect users with independent provider options where available. Providers are independent businesses.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use cookies, localStorage, analytics-related technologies, and similar tools to support site functionality, remember policy banner choices, understand basic website activity, and improve user experience.

5. Information sharing

Information may be shared with service providers, technology vendors, business partners, or independent provider options as needed to support user requests and website operations. We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and website integrity.

6. User responsibility

Users are responsible for reviewing and verifying any provider before hiring. This includes checking licensing, insurance, quote details, service terms, timelines, warranties, monitoring terms, and all project-specific requirements.

7. Data security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures may be used to help protect information. However, no website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

8. Third-party websites

This website may link to third-party websites or provider websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party websites.

9. Children’s privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the website after updates means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to hello@sentramatch.com.
