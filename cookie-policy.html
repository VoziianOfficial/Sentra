Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies may be used

This Cookie Policy explains how Sentra may use cookies, localStorage, pixels, analytics-related technologies, and similar tools on this website. These technologies may help the website function properly, remember user choices, improve usability, and understand basic website activity.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on a browser or device when a user visits a website. Similar technologies may include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, tags, scripts, and analytics tools.

2. Why this website may use cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support core website functionality, remember policy banner choices, help forms work correctly, improve user experience, measure general site activity, protect website security, and support operational needs.

3. Policy banner storage

This website may use localStorage to remember whether a visitor accepted or declined the policy banner. This helps prevent the banner from appearing repeatedly after a visitor has made a choice.

4. Analytics and performance

The website may use analytics-related technologies to understand general usage patterns, such as page visits, device type, approximate location signals, traffic sources, or interaction trends. This information may help improve website performance and content.

5. Advertising and conversion measurement

The website may use advertising or conversion measurement technologies to understand whether users interact with forms, links, buttons, or provider matching request paths. These tools may be provided by third-party platforms.

6. Third-party technologies

Third-party vendors, service providers, analytics providers, or advertising platforms may place or read cookies and similar technologies through the website. Their use of information may be governed by their own privacy and cookie policies.

7. Managing cookies

Users can usually control cookies through browser settings. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect certain website features, saved preferences, banner choices, or form-related functionality.

8. Provider matching context

Sentra is an independent provider matching platform. It does not install, repair, monitor, or perform security services directly. Cookies may support website functionality, but provider availability and service details vary by independent provider and location.

9. Updates to this policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the website after updates means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact
